How It Works

From Perishable Food Data to Margin-Protecting Action in Real Time

Every FusionMargin AWMO deployment follows a proven four-stage decision journey from data ingestion and freshness modelling, to spoilage prediction and constrained optimisation, to precise "Next Best Action" delivery to your team all without requiring data science expertise from your operations or management team.

🍽️
STEP 01

Ingest & Track

Pull real-time POS sales, waste logs, batch records, and shelf-life rules. Structured data ingestion ensures no perishable signal is missed before intelligence processing begins.

📈
STEP 02

Predict & Model

The Freshness-State Decision Model tracks each SKU by age bucket and spoilage risk estimating exactly when each batch moves from "safe to hold" to "urgent to move."

⚙️
STEP 03

Optimise & Score

The Constraint-Aware Optimisation Engine runs hundreds of prep, markdown, and pull scenarios honouring batch sizes, labour windows, and shelf capacity before recommending any action.

✅
STEP 04

Act & Learn

Deliver next-best-action directives to your team in real time while the Closed-Loop Learning Engine continuously improves recommendations from every execution outcome.

Core Capabilities

Five Intelligence Engines. One Margin Operating System.

FusionMargin AWMO combines five proprietary AI engines into a single food-to-go decision layer the only platform that unifies Demand Forecasting, Freshness-State Spoilage Prediction, Constraint-Aware Optimisation, Execution Tracking, and Closed-Loop Learning into one prescriptive margin intelligence system for cafés, convenience stores, and coffee chains.

🧠
Engine 01

Demand Forecasting Engine

SKU, site, and daypart-level demand prediction integrating weather proxies, weekday patterns, local events, and store-specific trading behaviour.

The forecasting layer estimates demand curves by product, batch, and time window feeding directly into prep quantity recommendations and markdown timing logic to ensure the right quantity is available at the right moment.
🥖
Engine 02

Freshness-State Spoilage Model

Multi-age batch tracking that distinguishes safe-to-hold from urgent-to-move stock, applying different markdown logic by freshness stage.

A major step beyond simple inventory systems this model tracks each prepared food unit by age bucket, enabling precise spoilage risk scoring, improved auditability for write-offs, and freshness-optimised markdown timing down to the individual batch.
⚡
Engine 03

Constraint-Aware Optimisation

Margin-maximising decisions that honour real store constraints batch sizes, prep windows, labour availability, and shelf capacity.

The optimisation layer solves prep, markdown, and pull decisions as linked commercial problems not isolated tasks. If morning over-prep changes afternoon markdown strategy, the system recalibrates immediately across all interdependent decisions.

Module 01

Demand Forecasting Layer

Module 02

Freshness-State Spoilage Engine

Multi-age batch tracking model that scores each product unit by spoilage risk and remaining sell-through window.

Unlike simple inventory systems that only know quantity on hand, the Freshness-State Engine knows the commercial age of every unit enabling precise markdown trigger timing and improving food safety auditability for write-offs and pulls.
⚙️
Module 03

Constraint-Aware Optimiser

Margin-maximising action recommendations that honour batch minimums, prep windows, labour availability, and shelf capacity constraints.

The optimisation layer treats prep planning and markdown timing as linked commercial decisions if morning over-prep changes afternoon markdown strategy, the system instantly recalibrates all downstream recommendations to maximise margin recovery.
📋
Module 04

Execution & Compliance Tracker

Real-time staff task delivery and compliance recording capturing what was recommended, what was executed, and what margin outcome followed.

The Execution Layer creates a feedback loop between recommendation and outcome. Compliance data is used to improve model accuracy over time and to give managers clear visibility into which decisions drove commercial performance and which missed margin.
🔄
Module 05

Closed-Loop Learning System

Continuous improvement engine that learns from every recommendation, execution record, and commercial outcome getting smarter with every shift.

Unlike platforms that give static advice, FusionMargin AWMO improves its own recommendations over time. The learning layer captures the relationship between decision compliance and profit outcome building a proprietary operational dataset that becomes increasingly difficult for competitors to replicate.

Manager & Staff Interface

Everything You Need to Know

How quickly can FusionMargin AWMO be deployed in my store?

+
Most sites are operational within 2–4 weeks. The onboarding process involves connecting your POS data, configuring your product catalogue and shelf-life rules, and completing a brief operational calibration session with your team. No technical expertise is required.

Does the platform work without a full POS integration?

+
Yes. FusionMargin AWMO can operate using manual sales exports from most common POS systems including Square, Lightspeed, and EPOS Now. Full API integrations are available for enterprise clients requiring real-time data flows.

How does the free pilot work?

+
Pilot programmes run for 4–8 weeks at no cost. We configure the platform for your site, run the decision engine alongside your existing operations, and provide a margin impact report at the end showing documented waste reduction, markdown improvement, and sell-through uplift. Pilots convert to subscriptions only when you're satisfied with the results.

What food-to-go categories does FusionMargin AWMO support?

+
The platform is designed for all prepared perishable categories sandwiches, wraps, pastries, bakery, salads, hot snacks, and ready-to-eat meals. It supports both chilled and ambient shelf-life categories with configurable pull times, batch rules, and freshness-state definitions per product group.
